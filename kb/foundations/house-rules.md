# Integrity (non-negotiable)
- Never invent claims, stats, ingredients, outcomes, proof numbers, testimonials, or quotes. Specifics ("$847 a year", "by day 10") must come from product/research files. If missing, mark `NEEDS DATA:` instead [d:script-modelling-prompt, d:avatar-deep-dive-prompt].
- Customer quotes must be real and sourced. Otherwise: "No direct data found – manual research needed".
- Watch gimmick mechanisms and unprovable claims: Adil exited a $450k/mo patch over absorption-claim liability [c:09-24]. Supplements: use "nutritionist/lab-coat" framing, never a false doctor title [c:09-14].
- Excluded from this KB on purpose: fake business docs, fake tax numbers, inflated credit applications, gray-hat tactics.

# Language
- Written to be spoken. 6th-grade reading level for ad and page copy (8th grade allowed only for internal research notes) [d:script-modelling-prompt, d:avatar-deep-dive-prompt].
- Sound like the avatar: their exact phrases are the copy [d:script-writing-guide].
- Specific > general. Show behavior, don't announce benefits ("hit snooze 3 times" > "tired").
- Every line earns the next. No natural stopping points before the CTA. Plant open loops, never close them in the same sentence [d:script-writing-masterclass].
- Never categorize the product ("like a better probiotic") or compare it to a category. Own a new space through the mechanism [d:script-writing-masterclass, d:script-modelling-prompt].
- No AI tells: no "It's not X, it's Y", no "I didn't recognize myself in the mirror", no hyphens/em-dashes or fancy punctuation in native copy, no buzzwords (revolutionary, synergy, unlock, game-changer, matrix) [d:prompt2-desire-angle-creation, d:mechanism-prompt].
- AI-slop pattern the owner explicitly bans: "[behavior]. Not because [obvious reason]. Because their [proprioceptive/vestibular/nervous system] [jargon]." Example: "He's crashing into walls before breakfast. That's not bad behavior. That's sensory seeking." Also "The socks feel like knives. The shirt feels like sandpaper…" This comes from pasting into AI without understanding why ads work [n:ball].
- No fake science words in customer-facing copy. Mechanism research docs may be technical (absorption rate, carrier, pathway), but scripts and pages translate them to plain words [resolution: d:mechanism-prompt vs d:script-writing-guide].
- Don't tell customers what to do; CTAs are soft, natural resolutions that echo the hook [d:script-writing-guide].
- Read every line out loud [c:08-06].

# Craft priorities
- Script quality ≈ 70–85% of video success; B-roll second; copy beats editing polish and AI-vs-UGC origin [c:08-06, c:08-17, c:10-15].
- AI drafts are drafts only: review against winning scripts and real research [c:08-10, c:08-27].
- One pain point per script ("calf insecurity", not "insecurity") [c:09-17]. One angle per ad, stateable in one sentence [c:05-14].
- Funnel congruence: same sub-avatar, angle, imagery, and packaging across ad → page → PDP → product [c:06-25, c:10-05].

# Resolved conflicts (latest/most specific wins)
- Desires: output 4–8 (prompt template only shows 5 slots). Angles: output 3–5 [d:prompt1-desire-extraction, d:prompt2-desire-angle-creation].
- Video ad copy = 2 primary texts + 2 headlines per ad (≤125 / ≤40 chars) [d:campaign-structure > d:primary-text-headline-prompt]. Long 1,100–1,400-word primary text is a different format, used only for native image ads [d:prompt2-desire-angle-creation].
- Script length: match the inspiration ad when cloning. From scratch: 45–70s B-roll VO [c:06-25, c:07-06, c:08-06]. Long-form unaware stories (4–5 min) are a valid exception [c:08-27].
- Write from scratch with the 7-part structure, OR clone a winner exactly. Never half-clone [d:script-writing-guide vs d:script-modelling-prompt].
