# Sequence
- Get a profitable ad first. Run it to the PDP, then test pre-landers/advertorials [c:06-29, c:07-09, c:08-24].
- Start LP split tests once sales are stable but hovering near break-even [c:09-14].
- Run ads to the PDP and the advertorial simultaneously; let Meta allocate [c:08-24].
- Test one offer across 2 congruent LPs [c:10-19].
- Listicle vs advertorial: one variable per batch [c:07-09].

# Match the page to the ad
- Start at the ad's awareness level and walk down the levels (table in foundations/awareness.md).
- Problem-aware ad → advertorial that deepens the problem and bridges to the solution. Never straight to a hard sales page [c:09-14].
- Product-aware ad → PDP with benefits + offer [c:07-09].
- Advertorials and listicles help explain complex or non-obvious benefits [c:08-06]. Listicle headlines are tailored per avatar [c:08-27].
- Most "low conversion with high CPM" is ad↔PDP incongruence: fix the PDP and offer first. $25–50 per angle is enough for early PDP tweak tests [c:08-31].

# Advertorial: 3 critical elements [d:landing-pages]
1. Hero image: reinforces the emotional state the ad created, continuing the story (struggle or transformation).
   - Represents the angle.
   - Instant pattern recognition from the ad.
   - Shows the person, problem, solution, or a symptom depending on level.
   - ❌ Generic product photo.
2. Headline (MOST IMPORTANT; test with live traffic): call out the exact pain/desire that got the click, in customer language, with curiosity or emotion. Templates:
   - "Why [PAIN POINT] Is Ruining [DESIRED OUTCOME] (And What Actually Works)"
   - "The Real Reason [TARGET AUDIENCE] Can't [DESIRED OUTCOME]"
   - "[NUMBER] [PAIN POINTS] That Keep [TARGET AUDIENCE] From [DESIRED OUTCOME]"
   - "Doctor Reveals: [Solution] That Is Helping [Demographic] Get Rid Of [Problem]". Supplements: nutritionist/lab-coat framing, not a false doctor claim [c:09-14].

   Generate 3–5.
3. Leading copy (first 150–300 words):
   - Agitate → validate ("not alone, not your fault") → introduce hope without revealing the solution.
   - Open on a specific scenario, use "you" language, "feel like reading their own thoughts".
   - ❌ Jumping to features.
- CTA early (sometimes halfway) and throughout long copy, because not everyone reads to the end [d:landing-pages, c:06-29, c:08-24]. Curiosity and emotion without clickbait.

# Levers by page
- Advertorial: offer, hero image, headline [c:06-29].
- PDP: hero image, offer [c:06-15]. Keep the mechanism light on the PDP [c:08-24].
- Early LP tests: change the headline and hero, not the whole page [c:10-08].

# Builders and templates
- Model competitor advertorial frameworks in GemPages [c:09-14]. PageFly's free plan allows 1 page, so mix builders [c:10-08]. Pagedeck (lps.pagedeck.com) for structures.
- Example pages: references/reference-pages.yaml.
- Packaging must look the same across ad, PDP, and the shipped product [c:10-05].

# From the owner's notes [n:ball]
- Tailor storefronts and advertorials per avatar or angle (e.g. one for adults, one for kids).
- Educate on the benefit of your angle; don't just hard-sell. Mine Reddit, reviews, and Quora for the copy.
