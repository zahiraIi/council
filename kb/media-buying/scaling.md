# When
- Scale only when new batches are taking spend [c:06-29].
- Latest rule: don't raise after just 1–2 strong days. Wait ~a week and judge on 7 days [c:10-19]. ⚠ was: double after 2–3 profitable days [c:09-14, c:09-17].

# How much
- Up to $1k/day: doubling is OK. Above $1k/day: +20–40% per raise [c:10-15].
- Performance drops over 7 days → descale 15% first [c:10-05].
- Surf scaling (repeated same-day raises, $2–3k → $10–15k) only for seasonal or BF peaks, and watch NET profit: one example did $24k revenue at a $300 loss [c:10-22].
- Ignore Meta's "raise budget" prompts [c:10-15].

# Structure at scale
- ABO → CBO once there are ~20+ ad sets [c:09-17]. Consolidating campaigns gave +15–20% at $20–30k/day [c:08-13].
- Above ~$3k/day, duplicated winners fatigue; you need new concepts [c:07-23]. Most stay in one format until ~$6–7k/day [c:10-12].
- ⚠ Video "needed" past ~$3k/day [c:08-06], but some brands do $100k/day on statics only [c:08-06, c:09-17]. Treat video as the expected path, not a law.

# Profit targets
- ≥ 5% front-end profit [c:10-05].
- 10–20% net while scaling is excellent [c:08-17].
- "Cash-flow printing" = break-even front-end, profitable on 3–6 month LTV [c:08-17]. Jordy ran a small front-end loss [c:08-27].
- Warning: 7–10% front-end can drop to 2–3% after payroll [c:10-22]. Track profit, not revenue.
