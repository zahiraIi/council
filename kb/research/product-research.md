# What to look for
- A new formulation or mechanism, OR an untapped avatar for an existing product. Low-traffic products are fine [c:07-13].
- Angles can beat products: track emerging problems/angles selling across several products (e.g. the parasite/gut-cleanse wave) [c:10-19].
- A new mechanism can win even in a declining trend, IF evergreen demand for the underlying problem is confirmed [c:06-29].
- Multi-avatar repositioning can reach $500K–1M/mo before you need a custom product (kids version; pillow sold on intimacy vs snoring) [c:07-23].
- Desire-driven products work when positioned creatively (pet dental care). Apparel and shapewear are hard [c:09-17].

- The stronger the desire for the problem to be solved, the easier it is to make work (hair regrowth, eczema, weight loss) [n:ball].
- "Everybody sells this product": (1) find a new avatar (easier), or (2) create a new mechanism (harder, e.g. creatine powder → gummies) [n:ball].

# Sources
- GetHookd; SimilarWeb or Trendtracker for competitor traffic [n:ball].
- Meta Ad Library (browse manually to dodge spy-tool saturation) + spy tools filtered to ads from the last 30–60 days [c:07-13, c:09-07].
- Amazon best sellers by keyword [c:09-07]; competitor ad libraries; daily dropship store accounts [c:07-20].
- Google Trends; Kalodata [c:08-06].
- Seasonal: TikTok/Meta searches like "Christmas gift ideas" [c:10-08]. Foreign markets (e.g. Korea) for new formats [c:10-05, c:10-08].

# Unit economics gate
- AOV $60+ ideal; bundles count (e.g. a supplement at $30–40/unit, $60–70 as a bundle) [n:ball].
- Minimum markup 3× (COGS + shipping) [n:ball]. Healthy supplement benchmark: AOV 6–10× COGS. Typical supplement: $8–10 cost → ~$60 price. Bulk ordering cuts 20–30% [c:06-18].
- Prefer light products < $15/unit landed (China often < $10). Avoid heavy or bulky items (shipping, price volatility) [c:08-13, c:09-17].
- High-ticket: watch tariffs, bulky shipping, CPA. Vacuums do well on Google Ads [c:08-31].
- Test budgets need AOV that can reach break-even CPA: $50/day can't at a $55–65 AOV [c:08-10].

# Process discipline
- Time-box research to ~1 week, then launch. Early "aha" feelings about a product are unreliable [c:08-10, c:09-17].
- Then run: validation.md → competitor-store-review.md → product blueprint (products/<p>/product.yaml, from [d:product-breakdown-template]).
