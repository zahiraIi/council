# Hiring order: editor → CS VA → strategist
- Editors:
  - Pay ranges from $10–25 up to $15–120 per batch. < 48h turnaround. $50 bonus per winning ad [c:07-16, c:07-27].
  - Sources: Video Editors of X community, ecomtalent.io [n:ecom-resources].
- Strategist timing varies: at $5–10k/day [c:06-29], or after profitability [c:09-21].
  - Adil's hire at ~$350k/mo spend underwhelmed [c:09-24]. Jordy promoted his editor at $500k/mo [c:08-27].
  - Best path: train editors into strategists [c:10-08].
- Telehealth/compliance hires ("legit scripting") only in regulated niches [c:07-13].
- Agencies: 1800DTC for brand agencies [n:ecom-resources]. For email, see email-retention.md.

# Payments and cash
- US LLC + processors early [c:06-15, c:07-27]. Multiple MIDs via Checkout Champ [c:07-20, c:08-13].
- Chargebacks < 1.5% [c:07-20].
- Cash reserve = 2–3 weeks of ad spend [c:08-10].

# Suppliers
- Bulk orders cut COGS 20–30% [c:06-18]. Custom formulation has an MOQ, so validate on a white-label product first [c:08-31].
- Watch supplier and legal risk on claims [c:08-13].

# Mindset
- Input goals (batches/week, ads launched) over revenue goals [c:08-03, c:09-21].
- Launch fast; don't over-polish [c:06-15].
