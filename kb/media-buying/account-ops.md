- Warm new accounts organically. Use older personal profiles; business-named profiles get banned [c:07-27, c:08-24].
- Build up to ~10 ad accounts over time. Warm with page-like campaigns [c:07-27, c:09-24].
- If metrics glitch (e.g. ATC but no sales recorded), switch to a fresh ad account on the same pixel [c:08-31].
- Founder-face ads aren't favored by the algorithm [c:07-27].
- Channels: Google Ads only after ~$12–15K/mo profit [c:09-17]. Snapchat is a growth channel [c:09-24]. Vacuums/high-ticket do well on Google [c:08-31].
- Compliance: Meta ad policy (health claims, before/after) applies to every ad. See house-rules.md integrity.
