Work top to bottom. For your own store, pull live data via Shopify first (products, variants/prices, sessions → ATC → checkout → purchase funnel). Each finding gets: severity (high/med/low), evidence, the KB rule it violates, and a specific fix.

1. Offer: tiers, savings framing, per-unit price, badges, gifts, guarantee (pages/offers.md). Offer beats design.
2. Hero image: matches the ad's angle and emotion; not a generic product shot.
3. Headline / above-the-fold copy: calls out the pain or desire in customer language.
4. Ad↔page congruence: same awareness level, sub-avatar, angle, imagery, and language (foundations/awareness.md).
5. Mechanism: present but light on the PDP; explained simply.
6. Social proof: real reviews matching the sub-avatar; testimonials congruent with the avatar [c:06-25].
7. Packaging consistency across ad, PDP, and product [c:10-05].
8. Checkout trust: logo, banner, shipping presentation, guarantee; diagnose ATC→purchase drop-off [c:10-19].
9. Tracking: pixel not duplicated (Omega/CAPI), campaigns optimized for purchase (not ATC), correct geo [c:07-23, c:10-19].
10. Email/SMS: abandonment flows, popup (mystery offer, 10% opt-in target, 20s+ delay, no smart triggers), order updates on day 3 and 6 [c:10-19, n:ecom-resources].
11. Simplicity: don't over-polish before traction; one hero SKU [c:06-15, c:07-06].

Benchmarks: Shopify CVR ~2% is a reasonable reference [c:10-15]. Most "high CPM, low CVR" is incongruence [c:08-31].

Store edits: propose diffs first. Write to Shopify only after explicit user confirmation.
