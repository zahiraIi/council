Inputs: transcript with timestamps (pull with youtube-transcript.io or similar [n:ecom-resources]). Product blueprint, avatar, and research are optional at this step.

Role: "senior direct response advertising strategist". Map the persuasion structure; do not rewrite, summarize, or improve it. Analyze only what is in the transcript, "not what we believe should be there."

# Step 1: classify (one type)
- Storytelling (state the level: Unaware / Problem / Solution)
- Educational/Mechanism (state the level)
- Product Aware Sales
- Offer ad (Most Aware: discounts, urgency)

# Step 2: extract
1. Awareness: where it enters, where it moves the viewer.
2. Framing: authority / personal story / demonstration / comparison / educational / data.
3. Emotional starting point the hook activates.
4. Belief shift: start belief → beliefs broken → end belief.
5. Mechanism: how it's introduced, what precedes it, the exact mechanism sentence.
6. Pacing: intensity curve, time before the product appears, product timestamp.
7. Objection handling: skepticism, price, failed solutions; where and how.

Also note (7-part teardown [c:05-14]):
- avatar, desire, angle (one sentence);
- hook type: authority / social proof / curiosity / pattern interrupt. The first 3s are the most replicable part;
- emotional tone in one word (set by music, pacing, grade, voice, word choice) and whether it matches the avatar.

# Step 3: name the framework
Examples: "Root Cause Emotional Story", "The Insider Secret", "The Failed Solutions List", "Silent Struggle Narrative". Add 1–2 lines on why it works.

# Step 4: section table
| # | Section label | Psychological job | ~Duration | Bridge to next |

Split by line breaks, else by sentence. It must be precise enough to rebuild the ad without watching it.

End with exactly: "Is this the framework and structure you want me to build a script for?" Don't write the script yet.
