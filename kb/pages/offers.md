# Offer types [d:offers-that-convert]
- BOGO: B1G1, B2G2, Buy 1 Get X% off. E.g. Resilia: Buy 1 / Buy 2 Get 1 Free (Most Popular) / Buy 3 Get 2 Free + free shipping; 33/55/60% savings.
- $/% discount: $50 off first purchase, $30 claimable coupon, 50% off today. E.g. Everyday Dose: $25 instead of $45, "$0.84/serving", subscription.
- Bundle packs (best for AOV): add low-COGS complementary items to sell the whole transformation (LED mask + gel + moisturizer). E.g. RoseSkinCo: 3 tiers at 15/20/25% off.
- Free gifts: e-books, cheap add-ons, warranties or purchase protection. Put them on higher tiers or use them to lift CVR. E.g. Hugterra: 53/59/63% off + three "FREE $25/$20/$19" gifts.
- Free shipping on higher tiers. E.g. Arctic Goddess: 52% off, countdown timer, free shipping from 2 jars, "Clinicians' Choice".
- Page patterns: Most Popular/Best Value badges, crossed-out price, savings %, per-unit or per-serving price, timers, gift cards with $ values.
- Winning stores mostly run 1 & 3-month supply tiers + subscription [d:reviewing-winning-stores].

# Economics
- Deep discounts and gifts raise ROAS but also raise break-even ROAS, which thins margin. Pre-sales, track ROAS; with sales, compare gross profit before vs after the new offer [d:offers-that-convert].
- Offer beats design: an offer change can lift CVR 30–40%+ [c:07-09]. Bundle + hero-image tests can double CVR [c:08-17].

# Testing offers
- Below ~$6–7k/day, split-test only 2 offers [c:06-25, c:07-09]. They must differ meaningfully (BOGO vs multi-month bundle + gifts) [c:09-07, c:09-17].
- A good ad still sells some units on a weak offer, which helps isolate offer vs creative [c:09-07].
- AOV levers: incentivized bundles, friction on the single unit, per-unit price display [c:07-09, c:08-17]. A/B price with the Kaching Bundles app or duplicate PDPs [c:08-10].
- Owner's store tests [n:ball]:
  - A "sold out" label on one bundle created urgency (good for toys and gifts near the holidays).
  - A free-shipping button on variant 2 beat the sold-out version.
  - Simple price changes, tracking CVR over a week.
  - Intelligems for big swings (hero image + price point).
  - Starter tests: price changes, BOGO, shipping thresholds.
- CVR unlocks in order of power: new angles from ads > consistent offer tests > congruent landing-page tests [n:ball].

# Cart and post-purchase AOV [n:ball]
- Post-purchase upsells (PPU): AfterSell by Rokt (Shopify app).
  - They show ONLY to customers paying in the store currency (USD store → US buyers) [img34].
  - Position PPUs as help, not generic discounts:
    - Quantity framed as the path to results ("best results after 90 days"; steep discount on the units they didn't buy).
    - A "special" discount ("you're our 50th customer today", "because you bought in our holiday sale").
    - A supplementary product they skipped ("we noticed you didn't opt for the cooling gel…").
  - Example: MilkyStrips upsell at $13.98 instead of $70 [img35].
- Cart (Upcart): shipping protection in cart, upsells of supplementary products, shipping progress bar only once the store has multiple SKUs (with one product, the threshold lives in the offer).
- Package protection: a nice AOV bump.
- Charging for shipping sometimes CONVERTS BETTER and can build trust [img36]:
  - Keep it under 10% of the product price (owner's note: ≤ $10).
  - Start low and raise it gradually.
  - Use free shipping on bigger bundles as the push up.
- Cart example (KittySpout via Upcart): free-shipping progress bar, free-gift lines at $0.00, "You might also like", and a package-protection toggle ("Free Returns + Package Protection, $7") ON by default [img37-39].

# Pricing
- Benchmark against top competitors, not Amazon (Amazon runs 20–50% lower). Adjust ±20% [c:10-12]. Don't undercut Amazon; brand and messaging carry price [c:08-03].
- Small price raises can be free (+$2 with no ROAS hit) [c:08-27].
- One hero SKU at launch; upsells and secondary SKUs go on the main PDP, not as separate items [c:08-03, c:10-05].

# Subscriptions
- They raise exit value (5k subscribers ≈ $200–300K; 2–3× multiples) [c:07-06].
- Churn: 30–40% at low scale [c:06-18]; >30% after month 1, sometimes 50%+ [c:10-08].
- Route cancellations through CS [c:07-23]. Ship monthly first, quarterly after the 2nd rebuild [c:07-20].
- You still need front-end profit at low spend [c:09-17]. Know the 2–3 month LTV before Q4 [c:10-08].
- Non-consumables: add recurring parts (filters, gels) or memberships; 3–6 month cycles beat annual [c:10-22].

# Holiday / BF
- Pre-test BF offers on Labor Day [c:09-24].
- Separate BF/holiday campaigns with sale banners 2.5–3 weeks before BF [c:10-05, c:10-08].
- Pre-orders with a ship date + discount [c:08-31].

# Checkout diagnostic
- Clicks → ATC → checkout → 0 purchases (e.g. 36 → 21 → 19 → 0): review the offer, shipping presentation, checkout logo/banner. Free shipping isn't always best [c:10-19].
