# Mass desire
- Copy cannot create desire; it channels an existing mass desire (shared by millions) onto the product. Desires shift direction over time. Find the strongest current one and focus it [b:schwartz].
- A mass desire is NOT a benefit. Test: Universal, Urgent, Repeating, Emotional. E.g. "I want to feel confident again", "feel like myself again", "avoid embarrassment" [d:prompt1-desire-extraction].
- Root drives to tie to: safety, recognition, belonging, control, freedom [d:prompt1-desire-extraction].
- "Market sophistication determines HOW you present the promise" [d:prompt1-desire-extraction].

# Sophistication stages (how many claims the market has heard)
1. First: state the claim simply.
2. Enlarge or outdo the claim.
3. Claims exhausted: introduce a new MECHANISM.
4. Mechanisms copied: elaborate or expand the mechanism.
5. Jaded: lead with IDENTIFICATION (the prospect's self-image), not claims.
- The course's 4-level version uses an acne cream example [n:ball img01]:
  - L1: "Clears your acne"
  - L2: "…in 2 weeks"
  - L3: "…by reducing oil production on skin" (mechanism introduced)
  - L4: "…with nano technology that lowers sebum production" (upgraded mechanism)
  Prospect skepticism rises with each level.
- Mechanisms are the main tool at stages 3–4 [d:what-are-mechanisms]. Most paid-social DTC niches (supplements, skincare, pet) sit at 3–5. Saturated markets are cracked by a novel mechanism, a new sub-avatar, or a new angle [c:06-25, c:06-29].

# Prospect's mind: desires, identifications (who they want to be seen as), beliefs.

# 7 techniques (use as a copy toolbox / review lens)
1. Intensification: make the desire vivid (concrete images, demonstration, accumulated satisfactions). → deep agitation, transformation scenes.
2. Identification: reader sees their chosen self. → sub-avatar congruence, native ads.
3. Gradualization: start with statements they instantly accept; chain acceptances. → belief shift sequencing.
4. Redefinition: remove objections by redefining the product.
5. Mechanization: verbal proof of HOW it works. → mechanism section.
6. Concentration: destroy the alternatives. → failed-solutions section.
7. Camouflage: ad takes editorial/news form. → advertorials, native image ads.
