# Awareness levels (canonical scale: use these 5 names everywhere)

| Level | Prospect state | Lead with | Product named | Page match |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Doesn't know their desire/need, or won't honestly admit it [d:market-awareness slide 2] | Identification with their world, story, "fun fact", curiosity; never the product | Late (≥ halfway / 30s+) | Advertorial that frames the problem → walks down every stage |
| Problem Aware | Feels the pain, doesn't know solutions | Name the pain in their words, agitate, blame-shift to hidden cause | Late (≥ halfway / 30s+) | Advertorial deepening the problem → bridge to solution. Never straight to hard PDP |
| Solution Aware | Knows solutions exist, tried some, not yours | "You've tried X, Y, Z" → why they failed → new mechanism | After root cause + mechanism | "Tried X/Y/Z" → root cause → new mechanism → product |
| Product Aware | Knows your product, not convinced | Superiority + proof, new mechanism, remove limitations; competitor downsides allowed | Early | PDP: benefits + offer |
| Most Aware | Wants it, needs a deal | Offer, price, urgency, guarantee, ratings | Immediately | PDP / offer page |

Rules
- The less aware the market, the less the hook talks about the product [b:schwartz].
- One script = one awareness level [d:script-writing-masterclass]. Page must START at the ad's level and move them down [d:landing-pages].
- Problem/Unaware hooks contain no product, category, or selling signal [d:script-modelling-prompt].
- Most winners are Problem or Solution Aware; Unaware storytelling has the biggest scale ceiling but is hardest [c:07-06, c:09-21, c:08-27].
- Static/image ads work at every level [c:06-18].

Mapping course labels → canonical
- Sub-Avatar Breakdown offers only Unaware/Problem/Solution (multi-select allowed).
- Prompts #1/#2 use Problem/Solution/Product.
- Script Modelling ad types: Storytelling (U/P/S), Educational/Mechanism (U/P/S), Product Aware Sales, Offer = Most Aware.
- Primary Text prompt plain labels: "just realized they have a problem" = Problem; "shopping for solutions" = Solution; "heard about us but not convinced" = Product; "ready to buy" = Most Aware.

Conflict note: Masterclass says "ESPECIALLY for higher awareness ads… introduce the solution LATER". Read as *lower/colder* awareness. Schwartz and the deck both put the product earlier as awareness rises.
