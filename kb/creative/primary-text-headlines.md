Format per ad: 2 primary texts (PT, ≤125 chars, sits above the video) + 2 headlines (HL, ≤40 chars, below it) [d:campaign-structure]. Each version uses a different angle or hook and comes with a 1-line note on the psychology used. "Sound natural, not like AI." Follow Schwartz awareness.

# Inputs (ask for any that are missing)
- PRODUCT: name, what it does, price, why it's different, end result.
- AUDIENCE: who, main problem, what they want, awareness level (see foundations/awareness.md for the plain-label mapping).
- VIDEO: first 3 seconds, main visual, captions y/n, vibe.
- CAMPAIGN: objective, desired action, current offer.
- PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGERS: pick 2–3. GAP: the course checklist of triggers is missing from the sources. Until it's found, use Schwartz's techniques (foundations/sophistication-desire.md) plus curiosity, specificity, social proof, urgency, identity.
- OTHER: competition level, brand voice, words to avoid.

# Rules
- Static copy tests: up to 3 PT variants with different hooks; headlines tested in pairs; description fixed [c:09-14].
- Long native primary text (up to 12 pages) acts as an advertorial, with CTAs throughout [c:06-29]. For native image ads use the 1,100–1,400-word story (angles/angles.md, step 2).
- Course prompt conflict: the opening says "3 versions" and the close says "3 PT + 2 HL". The 2PT/2HL campaign standard wins (house-rules.md).
