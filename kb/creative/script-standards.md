Score each item ✅/❌ with a one-line note. Output "OVERALL: X/Y", then each ❌ with a specific fix, ending with "Here are the X things to fix before this script is ready to film."

# 1. Awareness
- Hook matches the stated level. Problem Aware / Unaware: no product, category, or selling signal. Solution Aware may name failed solutions.
- One level throughout.
- Unaware / Problem Aware: product appears no earlier than halfway, ideally 30s+.

# 2. Hook (3–5s to earn it)
- Qualifies the avatar in 1–2s.
- Opens a curiosity loop.
- Specific emotion.
- Stops the scroll.
- Scannable in < 1s.
- "Lower resistance", not clickbait. Clickbait raises hook rate but tanks hold rate.
- Types that work: soft confession, open loop, belief shift.
- Clone mode: same type and function as the original.

# 3. Bridge
- Natural flow from hook to body, and from problem to product.
- Product discovery has its own mini-story. "Bad transitions are the #1 script killer."

# 4. Agitation (deep, not surface)
- Replays a moment rather than describing it.
- Reveals something the avatar wouldn't admit out loud.
- Shows the emotional cost of failures.
- Uses the avatar's own language.
- Would still be believable with no product in it.
- Surface vs deep: "I have chin hair" vs the 5:40am tweezers routine and flinching when her husband touches her face.

# 5. Failed solutions (if present)
- Shows the damage each one caused: the domino effect, each failure creating a new problem.
- Validates frustration: "You're not crazy for trying that."

# 6. Mechanism and product intro (5-step)
1. Shift blame ("what nobody explained to me").
2. The real reason past solutions failed.
3. Mechanism or ingredient first, product second.
4. Differentiate ("dissolve before they get past your stomach").
5. Product arrives via authority or personal discovery.

Also: root cause comes before the product, there is no category or competitor comparison, and it is positioned as the first or only solution.

# 7. Open loops
- Planted throughout, none answered early, no natural stopping points.

# 8. Language
- Spoken style, 6th grade, sounds like the avatar.
- Specific ("I woke up at 3am"); transformation shown through behavior.
- No fake science words, no AI tells (house-rules.md).
- Post-writing questions: Does the root cause make sense? Are you contradicting their beliefs? Is the emotional intensity accurate?

# 9. CTA
- A natural resolution that echoes the hook. Soft and inviting, not commanding.

# 10. Integrity
- Nothing fabricated.
- Clone mode: every section present, same order and timing, runtime matches.

# Post-launch diagnostics
- Hook rate and hold rate ≥ 30% are healthy. Strong hook + weak hold → fix the body copy [c:10-19].
