# Integrity
- Never invent facts, numbers, quotes, testimonials, reviews, studies, results or credentials. Unknown → `NEEDS DATA:`.
- Claims about what the product does must trace to `product.yaml` or a source the owner provided.
- No fake urgency, fake scarcity or invented compare-at prices.
- Follow the ad platform's policies for your category (health, finance, before/after claims).

# Language
- Write in the customer's own words from the avatar profile.
- Plain, specific, concrete. No filler, no hype words the customer wouldn't say.

# Conflicts
- When two rules conflict, the newer dated one wins; keep the old one as `⚠ was: <rule> [<source>]` so it's visible as history.
- Missing from your sources → write `GAP:` instead of making up a rule.
