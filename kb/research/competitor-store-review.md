# OWNER RULE (overrides everything below)
Competitor research is a MANUAL task the owner does personally. "DO NOT USE AI TO LOOK FOR THIS … just guide me on what i should do, do not attempt this part" [n:ball].
So Claude's role is to coach:
- give the checklist and what to look for;
- set up the template (product.yaml → competitors);
- review and analyze findings the owner brings back.
Claude does NOT browse competitor ads or stores, or build the ad bank. Exception: the owner explicitly asks Claude to look at one specific page they name.

# Where to find competitors
- Meta Ad Library, Google, TikTok, Amazon, IG Reels [d:competitor-research].
- Build your own ad bank with AdSpy, Atria, Meta Ad Library, Foreplay [n:ball].
- More sources: Trustpilot reviews, competitor ad comments, post-purchase surveys, Facebook groups [n:ball].
- Revenue estimate method 2: SimilarWeb or Trendtracker monthly visitors × 3% × AOV. Competitors worth modeling do ≥ $100k/month [n:ball]. Reference stores with visitor counts: references/winning-stores.yaml.

# Checklist per competitor
1. Revenue estimate: place an order for the cheapest item, check 24h later how far the order number moved, × AOV.
2. Reviews: off-site first (Amazon, Trustpilot, Reddit, forums), since on-site reviews can be fake. Mine detailed 2–3★ reviews for unmet needs.
3. Store: offers, images, landing copy. Which problems/desires do the images show? Which avatar is targeted?
4. Upsells and checkout. Advanced: buy, then study their email flows.
5. Unique mechanisms, especially if they sell the same product.
6. Ads: Meta Ad Library; AdSpy for high-engagement ads, formats, angles, comments. Save to Foreplay / Atria / Adslibrary.ai.
7. Landers: Pagedeck library (lps.pagedeck.com) for page structures.
8. Indirect competitors (same avatar/desire, different product, e.g. acne creams vs a red-light acne mask): borrow structures, frameworks, emotional tone, pacing.

# Reviewing winning stores [d:reviewing-winning-stores]
- 7-figure/month stores are often simple dropship-origin sites. "You don't need perfection to print."
- Review: offers (bundles, pricing, AOV), unique site features to copy, brand presentation.
- Pattern: 1 & 3-month supply tiers + subscription dominate. Free gift + quantity discounts for more branded stores.
- Examples: see references/winning-stores.yaml.
- Copy the winning store examples; don't over-polish before you have traction [c:06-15, c:07-06, c:07-20].
