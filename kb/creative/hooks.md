# Types
- By function: soft confession · open loop · belief shift [d:script-writing-masterclass].
- By framing: authority · social proof · curiosity · pattern interrupt [c:05-14]. Authority compounds with specifics ("one sniff" from a 30-year expert).
- Problem-aware mechanism hook: visible problem → blame shift to hidden cause → mechanism as the logical fix, with the product never named [d:mechanism-prompt].

# Examples (magnesium sleep patch, from the masterclass)
- ❌ Generic AI: "Struggling to sleep? This new sleep patch changed everything."
- Solution Aware: "$175 on melatonin, mouth tape, a sleep tracker… I almost laughed."
- Problem Aware: "Nobody talks about how broken sleep changes your personality…"
- Product Aware: social proof or story opener.
- Belief shift: "Your under-eye circles are not from lack of sleep. They're from a protein your body stops producing properly after 30."
- Open loop: the dentist and the gums. "Turns out the problem was not my sleep schedule."

# Bridges (hook → body)
- Problem Aware (bloating): "It started with my clothes… then dinner got really scary."
- Solution Aware: "£400 later… low-FODMAP, £55/month probiotics, 14 red flags… and worse."
- Product Aware: "Day 19… 'Who are you right now?'"

# Unaware hooks
- Broad, never mention the product. "Fun facts", origin stories, curiosity about their world [c:06-29, c:09-21].
- Study Eskiin and mushroom-coffee brands; source stories from TikTok UGC and Reddit.

# Production
- Write 3 hooks per script. For statics, up to 3 primary-text variants with different hooks [c:09-14].
- Don't do AI hook swaps that break the flow into the body [c:07-06].
