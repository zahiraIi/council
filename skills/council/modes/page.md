# Mode: page (advertorial, listicle, PDP copy)
Bundles: page. Live data: fetch the current page (own or competitor) when one is named. Artifacts: the paired ad (angle, awareness level, hook) and any draft page.

Roles:
1. Conversion copywriter (advertorials and listicles).
2. The avatar arriving from the ad: does this feel like a continuation of the ad?
3. CRO analyst: hero, headline, lead, CTA placement, offer, congruence.
4. Brand and integrity editor: claims, proof, compliance, house rules.

Member output: page type + hero direction + 3-5 headlines + 150-300-word lead + CTA plan, or a review with ranked fixes.
Rubric: pages/landing-pages.md (starts at the ad's awareness level and walks down; hero continues the ad's emotion; headline calls out the pain in customer language; lead = agitate → validate → hope without the reveal; early CTA) + house rules. Listicle structure is a GAP: reward responses that model a real example over invented "rules".
Chairman format: final hero direction, top 3 headlines ranked, lead copy, CTA plan, and next test to run.
Write-back: pages.yaml (draft); log.md.
