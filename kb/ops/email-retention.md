# Popups [n:ecom-resources]
- Start with a mystery-offer popup in Klaviyo; target a 10% opt-in.
- Delay 20+ seconds, no smart triggers (keeps bounce low).
- After ~1 month of data, trial Alia popups (30-day free trial). Keep Alia only if it beats the old popup by ≥1.5× using the same offer.

# Flows
- Simple flows from launch: welcome, abandoned cart and checkout, order updates (day 3 and day 6 post-purchase) [c:08-03, c:10-19].
- Outsource to an agency around $50–100k/mo revenue [c:08-03], $80k/mo [c:08-13], or $7–8k/day [c:10-19]. Example agency: Nest Marketing (for $50–100k MRR brands) [n:ecom-resources].
- Email images are usually 1:1 [n:ecom-resources].
