# Brief template [d:script-template]
```
AD NAME / BATCH:
Content library: <link>        Completed ad folder: <link>
Angle:            Format:            Inspiration ad reference(s):
Safe zone: keep all main content within 4:5; full video is 9:16.
VARIATION 1..3
| B-roll specifications | Script | Captions/overlays | Editor notes |
```

# Format guidance
- Video formats [n:ball]: raw UGC · AI voiceover + B-roll (most common) · subtitles + B-roll (captions + clips/overlays) · video carousels.
- Inspiration: videodatabase.io (IG/TikTok video database) [n:dash]; the Canva winning ads list [n:ball].
- B-roll + VO is the staple and top performer [c:07-06, c:07-09].
- Raw talking-head UGC is less consistent.
- Animation is a complement only.
- AI UGC works if high quality [c:07-13]; use real creators for authenticity niches [c:07-23]. Mixing AI and real footage cuts cost [c:10-12].
- No music in statics [c:09-17].

# Content sourcing path [n:ball img02]
- Beginner:
  - Rip content (Kalodata, Reddit, Amazon reviews, TikTok search) into a Google Drive ads + content library.
  - Test ripped and edited ads, review winners and angles, repeat.
- Intermediate:
  - Send the product to UGC creators (r/ugccreators, FB groups, Fiverr, Insense).
  - Send a B-roll shot list (based on winning ripped ads + research) and ask for a raw ad.
  - Keep the best performers on a low monthly retainer.

# Sourcing UGC
- ~$150 for 15+ shot angles [c:07-13] or $75–100 for 15–20 clips [c:08-10]. Ask for raw clips plus a shot list, not finished ads.
- Seed product to creators en masse; VAs can scrape creator lists.
- After a creator wins, run partnership ads (fewer rejections than whitelisting) [c:07-13, c:06-25].
- Hiring, pay, and turnaround for editors: see ops/operations.md.
