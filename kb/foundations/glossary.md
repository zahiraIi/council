- Avatar / sub-avatar: target customer / narrower segment with its own pain, desire, language. TAM: total addressable market (people).
- Mass desire: deep shared emotional want (not a benefit). Why-train: keep asking "why does that matter?" to reach the root desire [c:05-14].
- Angle: the complete story idea (trigger moment + desire + root cause + transformation) for one ad. Not just a hook.
- Mechanism: the specific reason the product works when everything else failed (ingredient / process / delivery / product type).
- Belief shift: reframing the doubt that blocks purchase. Failed Solution Domino Effect: each failed fix caused a new problem.
- Hook / hold rate: % who watch the first 3s / keep watching. Open loop: an unanswered question that pulls the viewer forward. Bridge: transition from hook into the body or product.
- Batch: ~3 ads tested together. Iteration: variant of a winner. Big swing: materially different concept, avatar, or offer.
- Ripped ad: competitor creative reused. Spy bank / swipe file: saved winners.
- Native ad: organic-looking post. B-roll + VO: footage + voiceover (the staple format). VSL: video sales letter. UGC / AI UGC. Partnership ad: run via a creator's handle (better than whitelisting).
- PDP: product detail page. Pre-lander: advertorial or listicle between ad and PDP. Hero image: first image above the fold.
- CBO / ABO: campaign- vs ad-set-level budget. Post-ID reuse: same post across ad sets to keep social proof.
- Surf scaling: repeated same-day budget raises (seasonal only). Descale: cut budget.
- ROAS, break-even ROAS, CPA, AOV, LTV, CVR, ATC (add to cart), front-end profit (first-order profit), rebuild (subscription renewal shipment), churn.
- Soft metrics: CTR, CPC, ATC. Hard metrics: purchases, ROAS (optimize for hard).
- 3×3: 3 ad sets × 3 creatives [n:ecom-resources]. 2PT/2HL: 2 primary texts + 2 headlines per ad [d:campaign-structure].
- MID: merchant account ID. MOQ: minimum order quantity. 3PL: fulfillment warehouse.
- Tools (corrected spellings): Kalodata, Insense, Nano Banana, Higgsfield, Foreplay, Atria, Adslibrary.ai, Pagedeck, GemPages, PageFly, Kaching Bundles, Klaviyo, Alia, Checkout Champ, AdSpy. "WAP/WP" is ambiguous in the summaries (?).
